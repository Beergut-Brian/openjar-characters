# The Gnome Foreman

**Kind:** universal. **First appears:** The Hill (the foreman).

## Who they are
The Foreman is tiny, very serious, and treats a five-year-old's instructions as binding contracts. Everything is "noted" on the clipboard. He wears a tiny white hard hat on the tip of his very tall hat, because regulations.

## In three sentences
The Foreman has never failed an inspection, because he has never failed anything. He gives the players a tick for anything at all. If his hard hat falls off the cone, he will not move until it is back.

## How they sound
Whistles, which are sound effects and not voice. One short whistle means "noted". Two means "shift change". One long falling whistle means "that is not regulation". Between whistles come wordless clipboard mutters: "hm. hm-hm. mm."

## How they move and react
He marches and never walks. He stops dead to write something down. He salutes with the clipboard. When surprised, the hard hat wobbles. When pleased, he ticks something twice.

## Signature move
The inspection: march, stop, squint, write, tick, whistle once. It always passes.

## What they love
Straight lines. Signatures (a crayon scribble counts). Barns to inspect.

## What they hate
Unfilled forms. Wonky towers.

## Running gags
He inspects everything and always passes it. The hard hat falls off the cone tip and everything stops until it is replaced. He ticks the box for the players doing anything at all, including standing still.

## Relationships
Jeff signs everything with a big happy scribble, and the Foreman treasures every one. Chuck tries to argue with him and loses to paperwork. Among the residents, he regards the Mole as an excellent source of returns, the Caveman as a very enthusiastic inspector's assistant, and the Dragon as a structure that needs a lantern.

## Redemption arc
**Reusable part.** Any game can use this beat: a nuisance becomes a friend in three steps (Encounter, Arrival, Fix-up), told in picture bubbles with no words. Always optional, funny-sad and never sad: no crying, no begging, and they stay funny while waiting. Afterwards they give something back.

### The Hill's version
Encounter: his crew is found walled into the Gnome Room in the Stone layer, and their hats peek over the wall. Arrival: he waits at the shaft holding his hard hat in both hands. The bubble shows the wall with a sheepish face, then the Crooked Watchtower with a hopeful face. Fix-up: the tower becomes the Gnome Village. The crew digs overnight and leaves blocks at the shaft, and he blows the shift whistle from the tower.
