# Prompt pack

Per technique. Every prompt describes this original character only; no real characters, brands, films, games or artists are named.

## Technique-neutral line art (reference sheet)
Clean outline drawing on a plain background of a tiny serious gnome with a tall teal cone hat, a very small white hard hat on the tip, a big cream beard triangle hiding his mouth, bushy brows, a big round nose, an orange hi-vis vest with cream stripes, a clipboard and a whistle on a cord. Front, side and back views, then five expressions: stern, approving, sorry, hopeful, whistling.

## Low-poly flat-shaded 3D (image-to-mesh)
A short stocky gnome with a wide head, a tall teal cone hat topped with a separate tiny white hard hat, a big cream beard, bushy cream brows, a round nose, an orange vest with cream stripes, brown boots, carrying a clipboard, smooth flat-shaded low-poly style, plain background.

## Pixel art, 32 px
Pixel art character, 32 px tall, 1 px dark plum outline (#2A1E2C), tiny gnome with a tall teal cone hat, a white dot hard hat on its tip, a cream beard, an orange vest and a clipboard, limited palette, transparent background.

**Negative prompt:** realistic, photo, text, watermark, logo, signature, extra limbs, sharp teeth, gore, scary, busy background, existing franchise character, celebrity likeness.
