# Chuck

**Kind:** universal. **Playable:** true. **Default for:** 2 (P2 starts as Chuck). **Body:** standard. **First appears:** Spaceballs (kicker).

## Who he is
> **PROPOSED by the maker profile — awaiting Kaden's approval or edits.**

A trucker who takes ball-kicking extremely seriously. Loud, overconfident, narrates his own
technique, and is wrong about most of it. His failures are funnier because he expected to succeed.
Secretly loves Jeff's enthusiasm and would never say so.

## In three sentences
Chuck has a trucker hat with a T-Rex on it and he will tell you the T-Rex is for intimidation.
He counts his steps on every run-up and gets a different number each time. When the ball goes
nowhere he blames the ball, the wind, or Jeff's vibes.

## How he talks
Fast, gruff, confident. "Watch this." "That one was the wind." "Stand back, Jeff." "I meant to do
that." Grudging praise: "...not bad." Stomps when idle. Yells at the ball mid-flight.

## Signature move
The stomp run-up: stiff-legged, boots thudding, hat flies off on every kick and lands back on his
head by itself. Celebration: a single fist pump and a nod, like it was never in doubt.

## What makes him laugh
He tries not to, but Jeff falling over gets him every time.

## Afraid of
Looking silly. Which is a problem, given everything.

## Relationships
Best friends with Jeff. Pretends it is a rivalry.
