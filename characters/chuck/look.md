# Chuck — look

## Must survive every art style
- Trucker hat with a T-Rex on the front panel. The hat is the silhouette.
- Black tie-dye shirt (black base, swirl in the player red and a little white).
- Blue jeans.
- Cowboy boots, brown, with a visible heel.
- Player colour: **red** accents — the hat's brim and panel edge, the tie-dye swirl, boot stitching.
- Stiff posture, chest out, chin up.

## Silhouette
Flat-topped hat with a curved brim, boxy torso, straight legs, heeled boots. Identifiable in outline alone.

## Face
Thick eyebrows, squint, determined mouth. Expressions: smug, outraged, grudging respect.

## Fixed colours
- Hat body: #2B2B33 (near-black) with the T-Rex in #FFD84D
- Shirt base: #1E1E26
- Jeans: #3B5BA5
- Boots: #8A5A2B
- Player red (accents): #E4393C

## Per-technique notes
- Low-poly 3D (Spaceballs): hat is a separate rigid body so it can fly off and return.
- Pixel art: 32 px tall; the hat brim must read at 16 px.
