# Boxbot

**Kind:** universal. **Playable:** true (proposed: Kaden's sheet 2026-11-10; playtest build). **Default for:** none. **Body:** standard. **Home game:** any game (Kaden's "Universal sillies" group: no home game). **First appears:** any game (Kaden's "Universal sillies" group: no home game).

## Who they are (Kaden's sheet, verbatim)
A kid playing a robot who never breaks character. Describes everything as a task. Sweet underneath the beeping.

## Arc (reusable in any game)
Boxbot is a kid playing a robot who never, ever breaks character. Every game is a list of tasks to complete, every feeling is a reading on a dial, and underneath the beeping is the sweetest player on the team.

## Game-agnostic behaviours
- **Winning:** "Task. Complete." A stiff robot dance, then a tiny un-robot hop it pretends didn't happen.
- **Losing:** "Error. Trying. Again. Boop." Presses a drawn-on button on the box to "reset".
- **Waiting:** Turns the drawn-on dials, says "beep" softly at intervals.

## How they talk
See `voice.md`. In short: flat robot monotone by an obvious kid, "beep" and "boop" said out loud, even pace with pauses

## Signature move
The stiff robot walk and the button press: one finger on a drawn button, "boop". Celebration: robot dance (arms at right angles).

## Afraid of
Rain. Cardboard.

## Relationships
Reports Big Tiny's "size levels" every time. Takes orders from Foreman Dot very literally. Thinks Zorp is a fellow machine.

## Home game: any game (Kaden's "Universal sillies" group: no home game)
Universal: any game. Good for tutorials and checklists ("Task: kick ball").
