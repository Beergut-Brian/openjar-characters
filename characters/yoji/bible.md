## Who he is (drafted from the sheet)
A Southern football fanatic who is the loudest fan in the stands and also, somehow, on the field. He calls every move a play ("That's a first down, partner!"), cheers for everybody including the other team, and talks tailgate the whole time. Big foam-finger energy. He got so excited on game day that he ran out of the house in his jersey, his hat and his lucky high-tops, and forgot his pants. He has not noticed. Nobody has the heart to tell him.

He is never mean. A fumble is "a great hustle play", a loss is "we'll get 'em next week", and the other team "played their hearts out, bless 'em".

## Arc (reusable in any game)
Yoji turns any game into game day. Kicking a ball, digging a tunnel, zapping junk: it is all football to him, and he is the commentator, the fan and the player at once. Whatever happens, he is already yelling the replay.

## Game-agnostic behaviours
- **Winning:** Hat in the air, both arms up, "YEEHAW! TOUCHDOWN!", then a little spike-the-ball stomp.
- **Losing:** Tips his hat to the winners: "Good game, y'all! We'll get 'em next week!"
- **Waiting:** Bounces on his toes, claps, does a stadium wave all by himself.
- **Oops:** "Fumble! That's alright, that's alright, pick it up!"

## How he talks
See `voice.md`. In short: Southern US, 30s, big warm booming fan voice; football play-by-play for everything; "partner", "y'all", "darlin' play"; never mean.

## Signature move
The touchdown celebration: both arms straight up like a referee, then a hat wave. Catchphrase: **"YEEHAW! TOUCHDOWN!"**

## Afraid of
The game ending. Also, a draft (nobody has told him why his legs are cold).

## Relationships
Cheers for Coach Whistle's every whistle and treats the Coach as his favourite ref. Calls Jeff "the best darn mascot in the league". Shares cowboy-boot talk with Chuck, though Yoji is strictly a high-tops man.

## Home game: universal
No home game: fits every game. In a kicking game he is the striker calling his own touchdowns; in a digging game every block is "yardage"; in a zapping game every hit is "a sack".
