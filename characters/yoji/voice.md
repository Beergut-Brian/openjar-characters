## Chatterbox voice design
Adult male voice, 30s, mid-low pitch, warm and booming, a friendly Southern US drawl (general Southern, not a caricature). Loud stadium-fan energy, big smile in the voice, fast excited pace that slows on the drawled vowels ("y'aaall"). Never sneering, never mean. No impressions of any real announcer, actor or athlete.

Post effect: none required. Optional faint crowd-roar sweetener under `win` and `cheer` only (separate SFX, credited if used).
