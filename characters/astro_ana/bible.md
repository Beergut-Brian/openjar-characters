# Astro Ana

**Kind:** universal. **Playable:** true (proposed: Kaden's sheet 2026-11-10; playtest build). **Default for:** none. **Body:** standard. **Home game:** Spaceballs. **First appears:** Spaceballs.

## Who they are (Kaden's sheet, verbatim)
Has trained her whole life to go to space and treats every round like a real mission. Counts down before everything, including lunch.

## Arc (reusable in any game)
Astro Ana has trained her whole life for space and treats every round as a real mission: checklists, countdowns, call signs. Every game becomes a launch, and every launch matters.

## Game-agnostic behaviours
- **Winning:** "Mission control, we have success!" Salute, then a slow zero-gravity spin.
- **Losing:** "Copy that. Abort. Resetting for next launch." One determined nod.
- **Waiting:** Runs a checklist on her wrist, counts down under her breath, starts again.

## How they talk
See `voice.md`. In short: clear, steady, confident, clipped mission-control phrasing over a slight radio crackle

## Signature move
The countdown: three fingers, two, one, point. Celebration: a crisp salute and a slow floaty spin.

## Afraid of
Scrubbed launches.

## Relationships
Envies and adores Zorp, who is from space and doesn't care. Counts down for Jeff and Chuck's kicks; Coach Whistle always blows the whistle on "two".

## Home game: Spaceballs
Spaceballs: counts down before every kick ("Three, two, one... KICK!") and calls liftoff when the ball goes up. Her dream is the ball's destination.
