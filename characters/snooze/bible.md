# Snooze

**Kind:** universal. **Playable:** true (proposed: Kaden's sheet 2026-11-10; playtest build). **Default for:** none. **Body:** standard. **Home game:** any game (Kaden's "Universal sillies" group: no home game). **First appears:** any game (Kaden's "Universal sillies" group: no home game).

## Who they are (Kaden's sheet, verbatim)
Half asleep at all times and somehow still good at everything. Nothing surprises or bothers them. Yawns at the most exciting moments.

## Arc (reusable in any game)
Snooze is half asleep at all times and somehow still good at everything. In any game they drift through, yawn at the big moment, and do the right thing anyway. Nothing surprises Snooze.

## Game-agnostic behaviours
- **Winning:** A long yawn, then: "...did we win? Nice." Pats the nightcap pom-pom.
- **Losing:** Same yawn, same smile: "Mm. Okay." Unbothered.
- **Waiting:** Dozes standing up; the pom-pom slowly droops; a tiny snore.

## How they talk
See `voice.md`. In short: soft, slow, drowsy, mid pitch; lines start or end in a yawn

## Signature move
The sleep-walk: eyes closed, arms slightly out, still on target. Celebration: a stretch and a yawn.

## Afraid of
Alarm clocks. Mildly.

## Relationships
Sleeps through El Burrito's entrances. Big Tiny makes a good pillow. Professor Fizz has tried to study how Snooze does it.

## Home game: any game (Kaden's "Universal sillies" group: no home game)
Universal: any game. Good for quiet moments and comic timing: yawns at the most exciting moment (Kaden's sheet).
