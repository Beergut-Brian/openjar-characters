# Snooze — voice

## Kaden's direction (verbatim)
Soft, slow and drowsy, mid pitch. Lines often start or end in a yawn.

## Sample lines (Kaden's, verbatim; use as test reads)
1. "*yawn* ...did we win?"
2. "Five more minutes."

## Chatterbox voice design
Soft, slow, drowsy voice, mid pitch, very low energy, relaxed and content; natural yawns at the start or end of lines. Gender-neutral.

Post effect: none.
