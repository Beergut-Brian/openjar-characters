# Rexy

**Kind:** universal. **Playable:** true (proposed: Kaden's sheet 2026-11-10; playtest build). **Default for:** none. **Body:** standard. **Home game:** any game (Kaden's "Universal sillies" group: no home game). **First appears:** any game (Kaden's "Universal sillies" group: no home game).

## Who they are (Kaden's sheet, verbatim)
Never takes the onesie off and answers most questions with a roar. Pure energy. A natural sidekick to Chuck and his T-Rex hat.

## Arc (reusable in any game)
Rexy never takes the dino onesie off and is a dinosaur in every way that counts, which is mostly roaring. In any game Rexy is pure energy: first to run, loudest to cheer, and Chuck's self-appointed sidekick.

## Game-agnostic behaviours
- **Winning:** Huge "RAWR!", stomps in a circle, tail wagging.
- **Losing:** One sad little "rawr...", then instantly: "Again! RAWR!"
- **Waiting:** Stomps on the spot, little T-Rex arms tucked up, practising roars quietly.

## How they talk
See `voice.md`. In short: high, loud kid voice in short bursts; roars are a kid saying "rawr"

## Signature move
The tiny-arms roar: elbows tucked, claws out, big "RAWR". Celebration: stomp circle.

## Afraid of
Naps.

## Relationships
A natural sidekick to Chuck and his T-Rex hat (Kaden's sheet): copies his stomp run-up. Asks Ribbit if frogs are dinosaurs. Best mates with Scrap.

## Home game: any game (Kaden's "Universal sillies" group: no home game)
Universal: any game. Especially good next to Chuck (matching T-Rex theme) and anywhere loud.
