# Big Tiny

**Kind:** universal. **Playable:** true (proposed: Kaden's sheet 2026-11-10; playtest build). **Default for:** none. **Body:** standard. **Home game:** Junk Yard Zap. **First appears:** Junk Yard Zap.

## Who they are (Kaden's sheet, verbatim)
A gentle giant who was a normal size before the grow ray. Not bothered by it at all. Careful with small things, apologises to anything he bumps into.

## Arc (reusable in any game)
Big Tiny used to be normal-sized, then the grow ray happened, and he is completely fine with it. In any game he is the gentlest one there: careful, polite, apologising to fences, and quietly enormous.

## Game-agnostic behaviours
- **Winning:** A slow, low rumbling chuckle and a very careful high-five, bending down so nobody gets hurt.
- **Losing:** "That's okay." Pats the ground, or the ball, kindly. Doesn't mind at all.
- **Waiting:** Sits down carefully, checks he hasn't sat on anything, apologises to it anyway.

## How they talk
See `voice.md`. In short: very deep, very slow, very soft; never raises his voice; a low rumbling chuckle

## Signature move
The careful step: one big foot lifted, a look down, then placed gently. Celebration: the slow two-handed wave.

## Afraid of
Stepping on something small.

## Relationships
Professor Fizz's grow ray made him big (Kaden's sheet says "the grow ray"); no hard feelings, she is his friend. Gives Mayor Pip and Scrap rides on his shoulders.

## Home game: Junk Yard Zap
Junk Yard Zap: the grow ray's most famous result. Can be the gentle giant who walks through the junkyard apologising to piles. Same gameplay collision as everyone: his size is visual only.
