# The Caveman

**Kind:** universal. **First appears:** The Hill (the museum keeper).

## Who they are
The Caveman is delighted and baffled by everything modern, and thrilled to be anywhere at all. He polishes treasures constantly. He has no words, only grunts and enthusiastic pointing. One ice cube is still frozen to the top of his head and it never melts.

## In three sentences
The Caveman was frozen in a puddle, thawed out, and decided the future is wonderful. He carries a soft polishing cloth on a stick, club-shaped, and uses it only for polishing. He is mildly suspicious of puddles.

## How they sound
Barks only, never words: "ooh!", "hah!", "hmm?", "buh?" and happy hoots. A gasp for wheels. A sneeze that comes out as snowflakes.

## How they move and react
A bow-legged bounce. He points with the whole arm. He gasps at wheels and opens and shuts every door he meets. He waves at his own reflection.

## Signature move
The polish: a gasp, a point, then a very careful rub with the cloth, ending in a proud grunt.

## What they love
Shiny things. Wheels. Doors. The Dump Truck, which baffles him completely.

## What they hate
Nothing. Puddles get a suspicious look, because he was frozen in one.

## Running gags
He polishes things that are not treasures, such as Chuck's hat and a cow. He sneezes snowflakes. He waves at his own reflection and is delighted when it waves back.

## Relationships
Jeff and the Caveman understand each other perfectly without a single word. Chuck wants his hat back, eventually. Among the residents, the Mole brings him shiny things, the Foreman lets him carry the clipboard for a moment, and the Dragon is the biggest thing he has ever polished.

## Redemption arc
**Reusable part.** Any game can use this beat: a nuisance becomes a friend in three steps (Encounter, Arrival, Fix-up), told in picture bubbles with no words. Always optional, funny-sad and never sad: no crying, no begging, and they stay funny while waiting. Afterwards they give something back.

### The Hill's version
Encounter: he is frozen in the Ice layer. He thaws, waves, and follows you to the elevator. Arrival: he waits at the shaft, beaming. The bubble shows the ice cube with a sheepish face, then the Flooded Cabin with a hopeful face. Fix-up: the cabin becomes the Museum. He displays and polishes every treasure, and shows how many are left in each layer.
