# The Mole

**Kind:** universal. **Playable:** false. **Default for:** none. **Body:** critter. **First appears:** The Hill (the shovel thief).

## Who they are
The Mole is nearsighted, polite and mortified by his own kleptomania. He does not mean to take things. His claws just close. Then he pops up somewhere else, squinting, and apologises to everyone, including the boulder he bumped into on the way.

## In three sentences
The Mole has never knowingly stolen anything and has returned everything he ever took. He holds his shovel upright in both claws like a bouquet, blade up beside his head. He would like you to know he is very, very sorry.

## How they sound
The "oh dear" family: soft, breathy, rising. "Oh dear." "Oh deary me." "Oh!" A small gasp when he is thanked. He never shouts.

## How they move and react
He shuffles, because he has no visible legs. He pops out of mounds, bumps into things and says sorry to them. He squints harder in bright sun, and his eyes only open, as two tiny dots, when he is startled. He falls in the pond a lot.

## Signature move
The pop-up: out of a mound, a squint, the shovel behind his back, and a very guilty apology.

## What they love
Tidy rows of found things. Returning things. Being thanked.

## What they hate
Bright sun. Being called a thief.

## Running gags
He apologises to objects. He returns things you did not know you had lost. He honks an empty vehicle and is startled by the sound.

## Relationships
Jeff thinks the Mole is a legend and offers him things to hold. Chuck checks his pockets and then feels bad about it. Among the residents, the Foreman gives him a tick for every return, the Caveman waves at him, and the Dragon snores near his burrow without noticing.

## Redemption arc
**Reusable part.** Any game can use this beat: a nuisance becomes a friend in three steps (Encounter, Arrival, Fix-up), told in picture bubbles with no words. Always optional, funny-sad and never sad: no crying, no begging, and they stay funny while waiting. Afterwards they give something back.

### The Hill's version
Encounter: he steals the shovel, pops up elsewhere, apologises, gives it back, three times. Arrival: he waits at the shaft holding the shovel like a bouquet. The bubble shows the shovel with a sheepish face, then the Old Shed with a hopeful face. Fix-up: the Old Shed becomes the Lost & Found. Tiles near buried treasure glow when he is near, and found items appear at his door.
