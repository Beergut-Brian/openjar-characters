# Coach Whistle — voice

## Kaden's direction (verbatim)
Loud, gravelly and upbeat. Everything is a pep talk. Punctuate lines with a short whistle blast sound effect.

## Sample lines (Kaden's, verbatim; use as test reads)
1. "That's what I'm talking about!"
2. "Shake it off! Kick it again!"

## Chatterbox voice design
Adult voice, mid-low pitch, gravelly and loud, upbeat pep-talk energy, medium-fast pace with punchy emphasis. Gender-neutral.

Post effect: a short referee-whistle blast SFX (about 0.25 s, separate file `sfx_whistle.ogg`) before or after most lines; the bark files include it.
