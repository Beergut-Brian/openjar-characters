# Old Reel

**Kind:** universal. **Playable:** true (proposed: Kaden's sheet 2026-11-10; playtest build: Kaden wrote this sheet and asked on 2026-11-10 to playtest the chooser with it tonight, which is the approval for this move). **Status:** proposed. **Default for:** none. **Body:** standard. **Home game:** Life of a Worm. **First appears:** Life of a Worm.

## Who they are (Kaden's sheet, verbatim)
A fisherman who has been after the same worm for years. The worm's funny nemesis in Life of a Worm, and a friendly playable everywhere else. Tells long stories that go nowhere.

## Arc (reusable in any game)
Old Reel is the one who let it get away. Years ago a bait worm wriggled off his hook into the dirt by the pond, and he has been telling the story ever since, each time a little bigger. In any game he is the friendly old-timer with a tall tale for every occasion, which never quite reaches its point and is somehow always true.

## Game-agnostic behaviours
- **Winning:** Chuckles, tips the bucket hat: "Heh. Well, would you look at that." Starts a story about a bigger win, back in the day, and trails off.
- **Losing:** "Heh. Almost had it that time." Exactly as cheerful as winning.
- **Waiting:** Casts an imaginary line, waits, reels in nothing, nods as if it were the plan.

## How they talk
See `voice.md`. In short: low, slow and creaky, a chuckle before most sentences, trailing off at the ends

## Signature move
The cast: a slow wind-up over the shoulder and a flick. Celebration: holds hands apart, "THIS big", wider each time.

## Afraid of
Catching the worm. He never did, and he's glad: then what would he talk about?

## Relationships
Grandfather of Bobber, his favourite audience. Swaps tall tales with Captain Crumbs. Bloom is on the worms' side and tells Old Reel off kindly; Old Reel tips the hat to her every time.

## Home game: Life of a Worm
The narrator. The whole game is his fish story, told from the end of a dock to his grandson Bobber, one short dock scene per growth stage: his hands spread wider every time ("it was THIS big"), Bobber less convinced. In the flashbacks he is young-ish Reel with a bait tin. He never appears in play as a hazard or a nemesis, and he is never food. He explains the mutation differently every time he's asked (a comet, his wife's chili, a funny-coloured rock); the truth, the pulsing Strangestone the bait tin sits on, is in the background and never said. (Kaden's Life of a Worm brief, 2026-11-10: "Old Reel's bible is adjusted to fit the dock scenes, not the other way round.")
