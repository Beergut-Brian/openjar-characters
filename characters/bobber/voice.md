# Bobber — voice

## Direction (from the approved bible, D89)
High, quick, a little scratchy, a kid's voice with real attitude but no meanness. Short lines. Catchphrase "Nuh-uh."

## Sample lines (use as test reads)
1. "Nuh-uh."
2. "Then where are WE sitting?"

## Voice design (ElevenLabs v3, then a saved library; see `references/voices.md`)
Child voice, male, about six years old. High pitch, quick pace, a little scratchy, bright and sure of himself: a kid with real
attitude who is never mean, never whiny and never bratty. Short punchy delivery; a doubtful drawn-out "Nuh-uh" with a rising
sceptical tone, then a quick grin in the voice. No impressions of any real child actor or character.

Post effect: none.
