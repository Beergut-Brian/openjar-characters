# Prompt pack

Per technique. Every prompt must describe this original character only; never reference real characters, brands, films,
games or artists by name. No logos anywhere.

Status: draft from the proposed look (Kaden's Life of a Worm brief 2026-11-10; bible approved D89).

## Technique-neutral line art (reference sheet)
Character reference sheet of an original cartoon character, a small cheerful boy of about six with a big round head, freckles, big round eyes and a gap-toothed grin, wearing a yellow rain hat a size too big (rounded crown, brim long at the back, low over his brow), a horizontally striped teal-and-cream t-shirt, rolled-up denim shorts with lighter cuffs, little brown sandals, holding a short blue kid's fishing rod with a cheese sandwich hanging on the hook. Front, side, back and three-quarter views, plus three expressions (gap-toothed grin, the sceptical squint with one eye closed, arms crossed saying "nuh-uh") and one action pose (small double fist pump). Plain white background, even lighting, no text.
