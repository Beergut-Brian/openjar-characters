# Bobber

**Kind:** universal. **Playable:** true (proposed: standard body, kid build; look pending Kaden's pick). **Default for:** none. **Body:** standard, kid build. **Home game:** Life of a Worm. **First appears:** Life of a Worm (every dock scene).

Design: Kaden's Life of a Worm brief 2026-11-10 ("The grandson is named Bobber. He is a new Jarling"). Bible approved by Kaden (Docket D89); look and voice are proposals for the character lane.

## Who they are
Old Reel's grandson. He sits on the end of the dock with his feet dangling, a sandwich on his hook, and listens to every one of Reel's tall tales while pretending not to believe a word.

## In three sentences
Bobber is about six, small, sharp and impossible to fool, or so he thinks. He says "nuh-uh" to every story and then asks for the next one. He is the audience the whole of Life of a Worm is told to, and the one who notices the clues in the background first (the ripple in the pond, the missing tree, the bite out of the moon).

## Arc (reusable in any game)
The sceptic who secretly hopes it's all true. In any game Bobber doubts out loud, then lights up when the impossible happens, and pretends he knew all along.

## Game-agnostic behaviours
- **Winning:** Fists up, then a cool shrug: "I knew that'd work."
- **Losing:** Crosses his arms: "That doesn't count." Then grins.
- **Waiting:** Swings his feet, squints at the horizon, checks his hook (still a sandwich).

## How they talk
High, quick, a little scratchy, a kid's voice with real attitude but no meanness. Short lines. His catchphrase is "Nuh-uh." Second line: "Then where are WE sitting?" Voice design follows `references/voices.md` after approval (child voice, male, about six; ElevenLabs v3 design, then a saved library).

## Signature move
The squint: one eye closed, head tilted, lips pressed, the face of a kid weighing whether a grown-up is lying. Celebration: a small double fist pump, then pretends it didn't happen.

## What makes them laugh
Burps. Grandpa getting the story wrong. The worm being bigger than the last time.

## Afraid of
Nothing, he says. (The dark under the dock, a bit.)

## Relationships
Old Reel's grandson and his only reliable audience. He calls him "Grandpa". He'd like the Mole, who apologises a lot, because Bobber is the opposite.
