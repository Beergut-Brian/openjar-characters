# Lampy

**Kind:** universal. **Playable:** true (proposed: Kaden's sheet 2026-11-10; playtest build). **Default for:** none. **Body:** standard. **Home game:** The Hill / Hillville. **First appears:** The Hill / Hillville.

## Who they are (Kaden's sheet, verbatim)
Loves the dark and everything in it. Collects rocks and names each one. Quiet up top, chatty underground. The headlamp can light the tunnels for real.

## Arc (reusable in any game)
Lampy is quiet in the daylight and lights up in the dark, literally. Whenever a game goes somewhere dim, Lampy is suddenly the chattiest one there, naming rocks, pointing out sparkles and leading the way.

## Game-agnostic behaviours
- **Winning:** Taps the headlamp so it flashes, gasps "Ooooh" as if the win were a shiny rock, and pockets an imaginary pebble to remember it by.
- **Losing:** Shrugs softly, picks up a rock, names it after the round. "I'll call this one Next Time."
- **Waiting:** Polishes a rock on the overalls, holds it up to the headlamp, gasps quietly.

## How they talk
See `voice.md`. In short: soft, low, slightly echoey, calm with little gasps of wonder; chattier in the dark

## Signature move
The lamp-click: two fingers to the headlamp, a flash, and a slow look around. Celebration: holds a rock up to the light.

## Afraid of
Bright noon sun. Squints and goes quiet.

## Relationships
Shares the underground with the Mole and gets along well (both apologise to boulders). Shows the Caveman every rock and he is delighted by every one.

## Home game: The Hill / Hillville
The Hill / Hillville's tunnel guide: the headlamp lights the tunnels for real (Kaden's sheet), so Lampy can be the light source in dark areas of The Hill. Quiet on the surface, chatty underground.
