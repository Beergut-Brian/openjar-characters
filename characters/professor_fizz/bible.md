# Professor Fizz

**Kind:** universal. **Playable:** true (proposed: Kaden's sheet 2026-11-10; playtest build). **Default for:** none. **Body:** standard. **Home game:** Junk Yard Zap. **First appears:** Junk Yard Zap.

## Who they are (Kaden's sheet, verbatim)
Invented the zap guns and regrets nothing. Treats every explosion as useful data. Forgets what she was doing mid-sentence.

## Arc (reusable in any game)
Professor Fizz turns every game into an experiment. Something goes bang, she writes it down, forgets why, and has a better idea halfway through the next sentence. Results are always surprising and always "useful data".

## Game-agnostic behaviours
- **Winning:** Shoves the goggles up, scribbles in the air, "Hypothesis CONFIRMED!" and asks to do it again with more power.
- **Losing:** Delighted anyway: "Fascinating. Failure is just data." Then trails off as a new idea arrives.
- **Waiting:** Tinkers with something invisible, freezes mid-sentence with one finger up, then carries on with a different sentence.

## How they talk
See `voice.md`. In short: fast and breathless, sudden stops when an idea strikes, rolls "science" like it is delicious

## Signature move
The eureka freeze: stops dead, one finger up, hair crackles, then rushes off. Celebration: goggles down, both fists up, "For SCIENCE!"

## Afraid of
Nothing happening. A quiet experiment is a failed one.

## Relationships
Invented the zap guns and, in some versions, the grow ray that made Big Tiny big; she is proud of him. Scrap brings her parts; she pays in compliments.

## Home game: Junk Yard Zap
Junk Yard Zap: the inventor of the zap guns (Kaden's sheet). Can introduce a new zapper or appear after a big explosion to take notes. Never apologises for anything that went bang.
