# Ribbit

**Kind:** universal. **Playable:** true (proposed: Kaden's sheet 2026-11-10; playtest build). **Default for:** none. **Body:** standard. **Home game:** Spaceballs. **First appears:** Spaceballs.

## Who they are (Kaden's sheet, verbatim)
Got hit by the frog curse and decided to keep it. Hops instead of walks. Easily distracted by flies. Calm about everything.

## Arc (reusable in any game)
Ribbit got hit by the frog curse and decided to keep it. Nothing fazes Ribbit; the only thing that can break the calm is a fly, and even that only for a moment.

## Game-agnostic behaviours
- **Winning:** A big happy hop and a slow, wide, pleased smile. "Ribbit. Nice."
- **Losing:** Totally calm: "Ribbit. It's fine." Sits down like a frog on a lily pad.
- **Waiting:** Crouches, watches the air, head snaps to follow an invisible fly, then relaxes.

## How they talk
See `voice.md`. In short: croaky and mellow, low for a kid, slow; "ribbit" instead of "um"

## Signature move
The fly-snap: head whips round, freezes, then "...never mind." Celebration: a big frog hop.

## Afraid of
Nothing. Mild worry about herons.

## Relationships
Rexy wants to know if Ribbit is a dinosaur too. Ribbit says "ribbit" and Rexy takes that as yes. Bloom names the flies for Ribbit.

## Home game: Spaceballs
Spaceballs: a calm kicker who hops into the run-up. Kaden's sheet says Ribbit hops instead of walks: in game that is a cosmetic hop on the shared `run` (see REPORT), so the gameplay stays the same as everyone's.
