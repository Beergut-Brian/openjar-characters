# Jeff

**Kind:** universal. **Playable:** true. **Default for:** 1 (P1 starts as Jeff). **Body:** standard. **First appears:** Spaceballs (kicker).

## Who he is
A fun-loving hippy who happens to be wearing a chicken costume. He is not in character as a chicken;
he just likes the costume. Barefoot by choice. Nothing bothers Jeff. Every kick, his or Chuck's, is
the best thing that has ever happened.

## In three sentences
Jeff found the chicken costume at a yard sale and has not taken the top half off since. He believes
the ball wants to go to space and he is just helping. He cheers louder for Chuck than for himself.

## How he talks
Slow, warm, delighted. "Whoa." "Far out." "Nice one, Chuck." "The ball is free now." Never sarcastic.
Chirps and hums when idle. Laughs at his own falls.

## Signature move
The windmill run-up: arms spinning, costume flapping, a little skip before the kick. When he
celebrates he flaps his costume wings and does a slow spin.

## What makes him laugh
Everything, but especially Chuck's hat flying off.

## Afraid of
Nothing. Mildly worried the ball will get lonely in space.

## Relationships
Best friends with Chuck. They disagree about everything and it never matters.
