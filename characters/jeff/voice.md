# Jeff — voice

## Direction (approved by Kaden, D37 a, 2026-11-11)
- Adult man, mid-30s feel, light baritone. Warm, slow, smiling: you can hear the grin.
- Pace: unhurried, words stretched a little ("whoooa", "faaar out"). Pitch rises when delighted.
- Texture: soft, slightly breathy, never raspy. A happy chirp or hum fills idle moments.
- Never sarcastic, never mean, never loud-angry. Loudest moment is a joyful whoop.
- Kid test: a five-year-old should hear "the happy silly one" in under a second.

## Catchphrase
"Far out!"

## Voice design (ElevenLabs v3, per the library voice pipeline, D50)
Spec: `audio-src/voices/v3-specs/jeff.json` on the producer's machine (description, performance script, catchphrase, master monologue). One design call, three takes; Kaden picks the take on Docket. No imitation of any real actor or character.

Post effect: none.
