# El Burrito

**Kind:** universal. **Playable:** true (proposed: Kaden's sheet 2026-11-10; playtest build). **Default for:** none. **Body:** standard. **Home game:** any game (Kaden's "Universal sillies" group: no home game). **First appears:** any game (Kaden's "Universal sillies" group: no home game).

## Who they are (Kaden's sheet, verbatim)
Extremely dramatic. Announces his own entrance, poses after every action and takes losing as a personal tragedy for about two seconds.

## Arc (reusable in any game)
El Burrito is the star of his own show and every game is the main event. He announces himself, poses after every action, and when he loses it is a tragedy of epic size that lasts about two seconds.

## Game-agnostic behaviours
- **Winning:** Climbs on anything, cape out, flexes: "EL BURRRRITO!" Poses three times.
- **Losing:** Falls to his knees: "NOOO!" Two seconds later: "El Burrito is fine. El Burrito meant to do that."
- **Waiting:** Practises poses, adjusts the cape, flexes at nobody.

## How they talk
See `voice.md`. In short: big, booming, announcer style, rolled Rs, third person, his own name stretched

## Signature move
The entrance: announces himself, cape swish, double flex. Celebration: three poses in a row.

## Afraid of
Nobody watching.

## Relationships
Rival to Sir Clank for most dramatic bow. Coach Whistle is his hype man. Snooze sleeps through his entrances and it wounds him.

## Home game: any game (Kaden's "Universal sillies" group: no home game)
Universal: any game. Best at entrances, round starts and victory screens.
