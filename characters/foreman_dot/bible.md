# Foreman Dot

**Kind:** universal. **Playable:** true (proposed: Kaden's sheet 2026-11-10; playtest build). **Default for:** none. **Body:** standard. **Home game:** The Hill / Hillville. **First appears:** The Hill / Hillville.

## Who they are (Kaden's sheet, verbatim)
The site lead. Bossy but kind, loves a plan, and celebrates every finished building like it is the first one ever built. Secretly afraid of worms.

## Arc (reusable in any game)
Dot arrives with a plan for everything and a clipboard of tasks. The plan always goes sideways, Dot shouts a new one, and the team pulls it off anyway. Every finish is celebrated like the first building ever built. Running gag: a worm turns up and the site lead hides behind whoever is nearest.

## Game-agnostic behaviours
- **Winning:** Throws both arms up, pushes the hard hat back off the eyes and declares the win "a fine piece of work", then starts planning the next one.
- **Losing:** One stern look at the result, a sharp nod, "Right. New plan." Never sulks; already pointing at the next job.
- **Waiting:** Taps a boot, checks an imaginary watch, nudges the hard hat back up when it slips over the eyes.

## How they talk
See `voice.md`. In short: loud, brisk, short orders, every one ending in a cheerful "chop chop!"

## Signature move
The point-and-stride: one arm straight out at the target, marching off before anyone else has moved. Celebration: a two-handed hard-hat tip.

## Afraid of
Worms. Kaden's sheet: secretly. Dot will deny it while standing on a crate.

## Relationships
Works with everyone in Hillville. Will want to compare clipboards with the Gnome Foreman (see REPORT: two foremen). Keeps a respectful distance from Ribbit's fly-catching, and a much bigger distance from any worm.

## Home game: The Hill / Hillville
Hillville's site lead: hands out the build orders and opens each finished building with a cheer. In The Hill, Dot can be the one who calls the next job on the board. Worm gag: if a worm pops out of the dirt, Dot leaps onto the nearest crate.
