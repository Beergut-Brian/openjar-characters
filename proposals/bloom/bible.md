# Bloom

**Kind:** universal. **Playable:** false (planned playable: Kaden's sheet; flips to true only when the 3D master, pixel form and voice exist and Kaden approves). **Default for:** none. **Body:** standard. **Home game:** Life of a Worm. **First appears:** Life of a Worm.

## Who they are (Kaden's sheet, verbatim)
A gardener who talks to her plants and is on the worms' side. Patient, encouraging and impossible to rush. Knows the name of every bug.

## Arc (reusable in any game)
Bloom is the gardener who believes everything grows if you give it time, including people. Patient, encouraging and impossible to rush, she turns every game into a little garden and every player into something to water.

## Game-agnostic behaviours
- **Winning:** Claps softly, hums, and tells the winner they "grew so much today".
- **Losing:** "Oh, that's alright. Seeds take time." Pats an imaginary seedling.
- **Waiting:** Hums, crouches to look at a bug, says its name.

## How they talk
See `voice.md`. In short: warm, sing-song, unhurried, mid-high; hums between lines

## Signature move
The watering-can tilt: a mimed watering of whoever needs it. Celebration: a slow twirl that makes the big hat flop.

## Afraid of
Stepping on a bug. Being rushed.

## Relationships
On the worms' side in Life of a Worm, so Old Reel's kindly rival. Names Ribbit's flies. Gives Granny Gravel flowers; gets imaginary cookies back.

## Home game: Life of a Worm
Life of a Worm: the gardener on the worms' side (Kaden's sheet). Good soil, good worms: she can protect or guide the worm, and hums when it is safe.
