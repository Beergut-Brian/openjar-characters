# Captain Crumbs

**Kind:** universal. **Playable:** false (planned playable: Kaden's sheet; flips to true only when the 3D master, pixel form and voice exist and Kaden approves). **Default for:** none. **Body:** standard. **Home game:** any game (Kaden's "Universal sillies" group: no home game). **First appears:** any game (Kaden's "Universal sillies" group: no home game).

## Who they are (Kaden's sheet, verbatim)
A pirate who gave up treasure for baking. Calls everyone "matey" and rates everything out of ten muffins. Generous, loud and usually dusted in flour.

## Arc (reusable in any game)
Captain Crumbs gave up treasure for baking and never looked back. Every game is a voyage and every good move earns a rating out of ten muffins. Generous, loud, and leaving a trail of flour behind.

## Game-agnostic behaviours
- **Winning:** Booming laugh, a puff of flour: "Arrr, ten muffins!" Hands out pretend pastries.
- **Losing:** "Arrr, a half-baked effort. Back in the oven, mateys!" Laughs anyway.
- **Waiting:** Kneads invisible dough, dusts his hands, a little flour puff.

## How they talk
See `voice.md`. In short: deep, hearty pirate growl with a friendly laugh; stretched "arrr"; calls everyone "matey"

## Signature move
The flour-clap: claps hands and a puff of flour goes up. Celebration: the ten-muffin salute (both hands spread, ten fingers).

## Afraid of
Soggy bottoms.

## Relationships
Trades tall tales with Old Reel. Sweet rivalry with Granny Gravel over whose cookies are better (hers are imaginary). Feeds everyone.

## Home game: any game (Kaden's "Universal sillies" group: no home game)
Universal: any game. Good for scoring moments (rates them out of ten muffins) and between-round snacks.
