# Scrap

**Kind:** universal. **Playable:** false (planned playable: Kaden's sheet; flips to true only when the 3D master, pixel form and voice exist and Kaden approves). **Default for:** none. **Body:** standard. **Home game:** Junk Yard Zap. **First appears:** Junk Yard Zap.

## Who they are (Kaden's sheet, verbatim)
Grew up in the junkyard and knows where everything is. Sees treasure in every pile. Fearless, scrappy and always a little sticky.

## Arc (reusable in any game)
Scrap knows every pile of junk by heart and sees a treasure in each one. In any game, Scrap finds the one useful thing nobody noticed, calls dibs on it and explains, with total confidence and no evidence, what it really is.

## Game-agnostic behaviours
- **Winning:** Jumps up and down calling dibs on the win, then holds up a bit of junk as "the trophy".
- **Losing:** Shrugs: "Doesn't count. That ball was broken. I'll fix it."
- **Waiting:** Digs in an imaginary pile, pulls something out, inspects it, pockets it.

## How they talk
See `voice.md`. In short: bright raspy kid, quick and confident about clearly made-up facts

## Signature move
The dibs-point: both hands shooting out at something, "Dibs!" Celebration: holds a found bit of junk overhead like a trophy.

## Afraid of
Things being thrown away.

## Relationships
The Professor's parts supplier. Best mates with Rexy (both small, both loud). Tells Zorp what everything is, wrongly, and Zorp believes it.

## Home game: Junk Yard Zap
Junk Yard Zap: grew up in the junkyard (Kaden's sheet). Can point players toward hidden parts or pop out of a junk pile shouting "Dibs!"
