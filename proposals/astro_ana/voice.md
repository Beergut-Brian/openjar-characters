## Chatterbox voice design
Adult voice, clear, steady and confident, mid pitch, clipped and precise mission-control phrasing, medium-fast pace, focused energy. Gender-neutral.

Post effect: slight radio crackle: band-pass about 300 Hz to 3.4 kHz, light noise bed, a short squelch click at the end of each line.
