# Mayor Pip

**Kind:** universal. **Playable:** false (planned playable: Kaden's sheet; flips to true only when the 3D master, pixel form and voice exist and Kaden approves). **Default for:** none. **Body:** standard. **Home game:** The Hill / Hillville. **First appears:** The Hill / Hillville.

## Who they are (Kaden's sheet, verbatim)
Takes his job extremely seriously for someone so small. Cuts a ribbon for everything, including sheds and holes. Easily flustered, quick to recover.

## Arc (reusable in any game)
Pip takes the office very seriously and the office is very small. Every event needs an opening ceremony; every ceremony goes slightly wrong; Pip recovers with a cough and a declaration. Runs on dignity, mostly borrowed from the hat.

## Game-agnostic behaviours
- **Winning:** Steps up on anything nearby and declares the win officially open, with a tiny snip of invisible scissors.
- **Losing:** Flustered for one beat (hat wobbles), then: "Ahem. A moral victory. I declare it."
- **Waiting:** Straightens the sash, then the hat, then the sash again. Clears throat.

## How they talk
See `voice.md`. In short: high and squeaky, trying to sound grand; slow and formal until surprised, then it falls apart

## Signature move
The ribbon cut: giant pretend scissors, a solemn pause, snip, then a startled hop when it actually works. Celebration: a deep formal bow that nearly costs the hat.

## Afraid of
Being overlooked. Literally: "Down here."

## Relationships
Officially opens everything Foreman Dot builds. Thinks Sir Clank is a real knight and has knighted him twice. Big Tiny is the only one who can see over Pip's hat.

## Home game: The Hill / Hillville
Hillville's mayor: cuts a ribbon on every finished building in The Hill / Hillville, including sheds and holes. Good for a ribbon-cut moment when a build completes.
