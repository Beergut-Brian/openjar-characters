# Granny Gravel

**Kind:** universal. **Playable:** false (planned playable: Kaden's sheet; flips to true only when the 3D master, pixel form and voice exist and Kaden approves). **Default for:** none. **Body:** standard. **Home game:** The Hill / Hillville. **First appears:** The Hill / Hillville.

## Who they are (Kaden's sheet, verbatim)
Sweet until she gets behind the wheel. Drives the Steamroller far too fast and denies it. Hands out imaginary cookies after every job.

## Arc (reusable in any game)
Granny Gravel is the sweetest person on any team until there is something to drive. She promises to go slowly, goes far too fast, flattens the job perfectly and denies all of it. Everyone gets an imaginary cookie afterwards.

## Game-agnostic behaviours
- **Winning:** Pulls the goggles down, does a little victory shimmy, and hands everyone an imaginary cookie.
- **Losing:** "Oh, well, never mind, dear," with a pat on the nearest shoulder; then mutters she'd have won if they'd let her drive.
- **Waiting:** Pulls the goggles down and up again, hums, revs an imaginary steering wheel.

## How they talk
See `voice.md`. In short: high, warm, a little wobbly; slow until she gets excited, then a gleeful cackle

## Signature move
Goggles down, both hands on an invisible wheel, cackle. Celebration: the imaginary-cookie hand-out, one for each player.

## Afraid of
Going slowly.

## Relationships
Everyone's granny. Feeds Captain Crumbs's cookie habit and thinks his muffins are nearly as good as her imaginary ones. Foreman Dot gives her the Steamroller keys with visible worry.

## Home game: The Hill / Hillville
Drives the Steamroller in The Hill / Hillville, far too fast: she can be the one who flattens a finished road or a cleared lot. Denies the speeding every time. After each job she hands out imaginary cookies.
