# The Mole: voice

## Voice direction
Small, soft, breathy and a little nasal, like a very polite person with a cold. Everything rises at the end. Never loud. The "oh dear" family is the whole vocabulary: no other words.

For Chatterbox: a synthetic voice designed from a text description. No real-person voice, no voice clone, and no celebrity or character imitation. Render each bark as a short clip, 0.3 to 3 seconds, mono, normalised, saved as `<id>.ogg`.
