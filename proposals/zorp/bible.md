# Zorp

**Kind:** universal. **Playable:** false (planned playable: Kaden's sheet; flips to true only when the 3D master, pixel form and voice exist and Kaden approves). **Default for:** none. **Body:** standard. **Home game:** Spaceballs. **First appears:** Spaceballs.

## Who they are (Kaden's sheet, verbatim)
An alien tourist visiting Earth and loving all of it. Fascinated by ordinary things like socks and puddles. Shows up once the ball reaches space.

## Arc (reusable in any game)
Zorp is a tourist from far away and every ordinary thing is the most wonderful thing Zorp has ever seen. In any game, Zorp turns up, marvels at the most normal object present, tries the word for it out loud, and loves it.

## Game-agnostic behaviours
- **Winning:** Antennae bounce, both hands to cheeks: "Is this... 'winning'? I love it."
- **Losing:** Delighted anyway: "On my planet, losing is also a gift." Takes a photo of it with an imaginary camera.
- **Waiting:** Pokes at something ordinary (a sock, a puddle, the ground) with wide eyes, antennae twitching.

## How they talk
See `voice.md`. In short: mid pitch, warbly and bubbly, stretched vowels, everyday words said as if for the first time

## Signature move
The antenna wobble: both antennae bob as Zorp leans in to inspect something. Celebration: a happy floaty twirl with arms out.

## Afraid of
Nothing on Earth. Mildly concerned about running out of new things.

## Relationships
Thinks Jeff's chicken costume is Jeff's real species and is very polite about it. Learns words from Scrap (wrong ones). Zorp and Astro Ana: she wants to go to space; Zorp is from there and finds that hilarious.

## Home game: Spaceballs
Spaceballs: shows up once the ball reaches space (Kaden's sheet). Zorp can float near the ball at the space altitude and react to it ("What a beautiful... 'ball'."), then cheer the kicker.
