# Sir Clank

**Kind:** universal. **Playable:** false (planned playable: Kaden's sheet; flips to true only when the 3D master, pixel form and voice exist and Kaden approves). **Default for:** none. **Body:** standard. **Home game:** any game (Kaden's "Universal sillies" group: no home game). **First appears:** any game (Kaden's "Universal sillies" group: no home game).

## Who they are (Kaden's sheet, verbatim)
Fully believes he is a real knight. Brave, polite and wrong about most things. Bows before every task.

## Arc (reusable in any game)
Sir Clank fully believes he is a real knight on a real quest, and every game is a noble deed. He bows, he charges, he is wrong about what is happening, and he is so polite about it that it works out anyway.

## Game-agnostic behaviours
- **Winning:** A deep bow to the opponent first, then sword-hand to the sky: "Victory, with honour!" The pot rings.
- **Losing:** Kneels, hand on heart: "A worthy foe. I shall return." Bows again.
- **Waiting:** Stands at attention, very straight, adjusts the pot so the handle points exactly backwards.

## How they talk
See `voice.md`. In short: mid-low and theatrical, formal old-timey phrasing, a slight metallic ring

## Signature move
The pre-task bow: hand on chest, deep bow, the pot slides forward, pushed back with dignity. Celebration: a pretend sword salute.

## Afraid of
Being impolite. Dragons, in theory; he has met the Dragon in The Hill and decided it is a noble steed.

## Relationships
Has been knighted twice by Mayor Pip. Treats Granny Gravel as a queen. Sworn protector of Ribbit, "the cursed one".

## Home game: any game (Kaden's "Universal sillies" group: no home game)
Universal: any game. Good for openings (bows before the round), protecting someone smaller, or charging bravely in the wrong direction.
