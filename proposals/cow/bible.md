## Who she is
Patience is a black-and-white cow who has seen it all and is bothered by none of it. Zap her huge and she chews. Shrink her to the size of a teacup and she chews. Glue her to a car and she becomes the car's most patient passenger. She does not run from the players and she does not fight back. She waits, chewing, with heavy-lidded eyes that say "go on then".

## In three sentences
Patience always ends up somewhere improbable (in the pond, on the barn roof, on top of the tower) and always looks as if she meant to be there. Tipped onto her side, she lets out one long, put-upon moo and waits to be set right. The only thing her patience does not cover is being lifted off the ground.

## How she sounds
Moos only, never words. A low, warm, unhurried "mmmooo" for almost everything, a long sighing moo when tipped, a rising "moo?" when something new happens to her, and one high, wobbly moo when she is ballooned. See `voice.md`. In Junk Yard Zap her pitch follows her size (a giant Patience moos low, a tiny one squeaks).

## How she moves and reacts
Slow and steady: an idle chew and a tail that flicks the flies away. When she does move it is a bouncy little trot. She reacts to every gun with the yard's reaction faces:
- **Grabbed** (gravity grab): wide-eyed, legs dangling, then neutral again: she has decided this is fine.
- **Thrown, tipped:** dizzy, on her side with her legs stuck out (`tipped`), one long sigh-moo.
- **Bouncy:** smug. She rather likes it.
- **Ballooned:** panicked, the only time. Patience runs out at about six metres.
- **Frozen:** her eyes keep moving inside the ice, still chewing.
- **Pond:** she stands in it, chewing, as if it were her idea.

## Signature move
The slow blink: half-lidded eyes close, open, and she goes back to chewing. Celebration: one happy hop (`cheer`), all four hooves off the ground, then straight back to grazing as if it never happened.

## What she loves
Grass, the shade of the barn, being glued to things that go somewhere, and the pond on a hot day.

## What she hates
Heights. Balloons. Being rushed (she will not be rushed).

## Running gags
- She is never where you left her. The yard saves her where she ends up (every zapped cow stays zapped), so she is still on the roof next time.
- Whatever happens, she chews. Giant, tiny, frozen, upside down: the jaw keeps going.
- The one balloon moo. Every other reaction is calm, so the panicked one always gets the laugh.

## Relationships
Jeff thinks she is the best thing in the yard and keeps making her enormous. Chuck tips her over "to see what happens" and then helps her up. She lets the chickens sit on her back. In The Hill she is one of the cows that wander out of the barns.

## As a background character (any game)
She works with no lines and no story: grazing at the edge of a field (`graze`), sitting and watching the players (`sit`, smug), trotting out of a barn (`run`), one moo at the wrong moment. She is never the obstacle and never the goal-keeper; she is the scenery that reacts.
