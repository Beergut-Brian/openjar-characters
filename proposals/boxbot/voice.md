# Boxbot — voice

## Kaden's direction (verbatim)
Flat robot monotone done by an obvious kid, with "beep" and "boop" said out loud. Even pace, small pauses between words.

## Sample lines (Kaden's, verbatim; use as test reads)
1. "Beep. Task. Complete. Boop."
2. "Fun levels at one hundred percent."

## Chatterbox voice design
Kid voice doing a flat robot monotone, mid pitch, even and deliberate with small pauses between words; "beep" and "boop" spoken, not synthesised. Gender-neutral.

Post effect: none (the robot is the kid's acting, not an effect; at most a very light bit-crush, off by default).
