## Chatterbox voice design
Soft, low, gentle voice with a calm, unhurried pace; small breathy gasps of wonder; slightly hollow and reverberant as if in a cave. Gender-neutral.

Post effect: light cave reverb (short, about 0.6 s tail, low mix) on every line.
