# Coach Whistle

**Kind:** universal. **Playable:** false (planned playable: Kaden's sheet; flips to true only when the 3D master, pixel form and voice exist and Kaden approves). **Default for:** none. **Body:** standard. **Home game:** Spaceballs. **First appears:** Spaceballs.

## Who they are (Kaden's sheet, verbatim)
Believes in you more than you believe in yourself. Every kick is the greatest kick in history. Has never once explained the rules.

## Arc (reusable in any game)
Coach Whistle has never explained the rules and never needs to. Whatever happens, it was the greatest effort in history, and the Coach is already yelling encouragement for the next one. Belief is the whole playbook.

## Game-agnostic behaviours
- **Winning:** Whistle blast, both arms up, "THAT'S what I'm talking about!", and a chest bump with anyone in reach.
- **Losing:** Whistle blast, clap clap: "Shake it off! Go again!" Genuinely more excited than before.
- **Waiting:** Jogs on the spot, claps, gives a tiny test toot on the whistle.

## How they talk
See `voice.md`. In short: loud, gravelly, upbeat; everything a pep talk; lines punctuated with a whistle blast

## Signature move
The whistle-and-point: whistle in the teeth, two-finger point at you, blast. Celebration: a stomping fist-pump lap.

## Afraid of
Silence. And rulebooks.

## Relationships
Jeff's biggest fan and Chuck's favourite to argue with (Chuck wants the rules; Coach has none). Counts down with Astro Ana, badly.

## Home game: Spaceballs
Spaceballs: the sideline coach. Yells encouragement before every kick and calls each one the greatest kick in history. Whistle blast on every launch.
