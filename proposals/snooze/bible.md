## Game-agnostic behaviours
- **Winning:** A long yawn, then: "...did we win? Nice." Pats the nightcap pom-pom.
- **Losing:** Same yawn, same smile: "Mm. Okay." Unbothered.
- **Waiting:** Dozes standing up; the pom-pom slowly droops; a tiny snore.
